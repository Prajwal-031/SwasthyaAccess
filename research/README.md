# HCI research materials

This directory is a starter, not an approved human-subjects protocol. Obtain institutional
review/ethics approval where required before recruiting participants or collecting data.

Suggested study: within-subject comparison of a conventional interface and SwasthyaAccess.
Counterbalance order, use equivalent tasks and fictional data, and pilot the protocol first.

Collect task completion, time, errors, moderator assistance, SUS, NASA-TLX and short
interviews. Operationalize low digital literacy with a short neutral screening measure;
do not infer it from education, age or English fluency. Record language/accent/noise context
without collecting unnecessary identifiers.
